> info: 48-solfege
notes and intervals on this website are mostly written in 48-solfege. notes are assigned a consonant based on what 12 EDO note they are closest to, and assigned one of five vowels based how far away it's tuned from that note.
the vowels are spaced at 25 cent intervals, or, quarters of a semitone. a number at the end represents the octave the note is in. if denoting an interval, the name of the interval is the high note given that the low note is da1. therefore, a perfect fifth would be written sa1. a sharp fifth would be se1... and so on.
